## Download for Mac

| Your Mac | Download |
|---|---|
| Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4 and later) | **GravelMaps-arm64.dmg** |
| Intel | **GravelMaps-x86_64.dmg** |

Not sure which you have? Apple menu → **About This Mac**: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple Silicon, "Processor: Intel" means Intel.

**Installing:** open the DMG and drag GravelMaps onto Applications. The first time you open it, macOS
says it can't verify the app (GravelMaps is free and not registered with Apple). Go to
**System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once.

**Already have GravelMaps?** It updates itself: look for "New version available" in the app.
