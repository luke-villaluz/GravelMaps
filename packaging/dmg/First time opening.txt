Installing GravelMaps
=====================

1. Drag GravelMaps onto the Applications folder in this window.
2. Open GravelMaps from Applications (or Launchpad).

The first time you open it, macOS says it "cannot verify" GravelMaps and won't open it.
That's because GravelMaps is a free app that isn't registered with Apple. To open it:

  1. Click "Done" on that message.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the message about GravelMaps and click "Open Anyway".
  4. Click "Open Anyway" again, and enter your Mac password if asked.

You only do this once. After that it opens like any other app, and updates install themselves.

To keep it in your Dock: while it's open, right-click its Dock icon, then Options, then Keep in Dock.

On first launch, pick your states. GravelMaps downloads the maps once (about 1-2 GB per state,
10-30 minutes), and from then on everything works without internet.
